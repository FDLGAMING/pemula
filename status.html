THE STORY BEHIND SILENT BAHAR

WHO IS BEHIND SILENT BAHAR?

My name is Fadil Darmawan. Silent Bahar is more than just a username or a website. It is a personal identity that represents my interests, ideas, experiments, and the things I create.

I am interested in programming, web development, technology, gaming, and music. I started learning by experimenting with things myself, making mistakes, fixing them, and trying again.

WHERE DID "SILENT BAHAR" COME FROM?

Silent Bahar became the name I chose to represent myself on the internet.

The name became a digital identity where I could put my projects, music interests, experiments, and ideas in one place.

Over time, Silent Bahar became more than a name. It became a place where my journey could be recorded.

WHY WAS THIS WEBSITE CREATED?

This website started from a simple idea: creating a personal place on the internet that represents me.

Instead of only having social media links, I wanted something that could show who I am, what I build, what music I like, and what projects I have worked on.

The website continues to evolve as I learn more. New pages, ideas, and projects can be added over time.

THE JOURNEY OF BUILDING IT

One of the interesting parts of this project is how it was built. A large part of the development was done from an Android phone.

There were plenty of errors, broken buttons, misplaced code, and moments where something worked and then suddenly broke again.

The hamburger menu, the close button, page navigation, About page, Music page, and Projects page were all developed step by step through experimentation.

ChatGPT became a development collaborator throughout the process, helping with ideas, code, debugging, structure, and improvements.

FROM A SIMPLE PAGE TO A PERSONAL WEBSITE

What started as a simple page with a flower and social media links slowly became something bigger.

Now Silent Bahar has its own Home, About, Music, Projects, and Status pages.

The project is still unfinished — and that's the point. There is always something new to learn and something new to build.
